# Literature Review

This folder collects research literature relevant to the closed-loop precision stepper project. It is intentionally broad: papers are grouped by direct relevance to the prototype and by adjacent topics that may affect the error budget, mechanical design, controller, or measurement method.

## Scope

The review covers:

- Hybrid and permanent-magnet stepper-motor modelling.
- Microstepping, torque modulation, nonlinearities, and shaft motion.
- Closed-loop stepper control and position regulation.
- Rotary encoder calibration, installation error, runout, and uncertainty.
- Precision rotary stages and angular positioning metrology.
- Harmonic, planetary, and other transmission error mechanisms.
- Timing-belt transmission error, compliance, slip, and control.
- Hysteresis, friction, and compensation methods.
- Variable-ratio transmissions and why they may complicate precision positioning.

## How To Use This Index

The links are starting points for the literature review, not evidence that every claim in a paper applies directly to this project. Before using a result in the final report:

1. Download and read the full paper where possible.
2. Record the exact method, hardware, operating range, and uncertainty.
3. Separate the paper's measured result from our interpretation.
4. Check whether the result concerns resolution, accuracy, repeatability, backlash, transmission error, or control error.
5. Record any useful equation, figure, dataset, or experimental procedure in the project evidence index.

Search results and metadata were collected on 2026-08-20. Some publisher pages may require institutional access. Open-access alternatives are included where found.

## Priority Labels

- **Core:** directly informs the present motor, encoder, transmission, or benchmark design.
- **Supporting:** technically relevant but not necessarily transferable to the prototype.
- **Adjacent:** useful for broader design context or future alternatives.
- **Verify:** title/link found during discovery; full bibliographic details and applicability still need checking.
